Ellis

say it without saying it

or join a friend

  1. One player is secretly chosen each round and sees a word or two.
  2. They write clues in the chat to get the others to write that word. Saying the word, any part of it, or any extra blocked word is not allowed.
  3. Every time someone writes the word, the chosen player gets a new one.
  4. When time runs out, everyone is revealed and the chosen player gets points for the words found.
  5. Players take turns being the chosen one over all the rounds. Highest total wins.
